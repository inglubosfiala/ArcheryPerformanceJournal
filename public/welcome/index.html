Your score only tells you what happened.

A 9 can come from a perfect shot the wind moved. A 10 can come from a rushed shot that got lucky. If you only track points, you train the result, not the process that makes it.

Most archers juggle a scoring app, an interval timer for holds and a notebook for the mental game. Nothing connects them, so nothing tells you why a day went well.

Score

Every arrow, in three taps.

Touch the target face (a magnifier shows the exact spot) or tap X 10 9 8 7 6 M. Let-downs, a note per end, sight marks per distance and your gear setup are saved with each session. Close the app mid-end and it picks up exactly where you left off.

Mind

Grade the shot, not just the score.

After every arrow, grade two things from 1 (best) to 5:

  • M — mental program: did you run your routine and keep your focus?
  • S — shooting cycle: did the technique hold together?

Then the journal shows what no score sheet can: your average arrow at each mental grade. Once you see what a lost routine costs you, you stop hoping for good days and start building them.

Program % is the share of arrows shot with your full mental program (M1–2). It's the number to raise first. You can also log mental-only sessions: visualisation, breathing, shot routine, relaxation, blank boss, self-talk.

Body

A built-in SPT timer for holds.

Set the hold, rest, reps and sets. Clear prepare, hold and rest phases count you through every rep, so you watch the bow, not the clock. Each SPT session goes into the same journal as your scores, so your whole training load is in one place.

Train on the days you can't reach the range: in winter, on rest days, when you travel.

See

Your phone sees your arrows. Live.

Point the camera at the target. The app finds the face, reads where each arrow landed and puts the score next to the hit, in real time. Hold steady for a second and it takes the end; move any arrow with one touch if it read one wrong.

Frames are analysed on your phone and thrown away. Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Works best on standard target faces in good light.

Insights

Score, mind and body in one view.

Trends over 30 days, 90 days, a year or all time, filtered by distance and session type. Average arrow, Avg M, Avg S, program % and training minutes side by side, so you spot the weeks where the process slipped before the score did.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free. Coffees cover the hosting and the time.

Which bows does it support?

It was built for Olympic recurve and works for any bow shooting at standard target faces: compound, barebow, longbow.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once it's installed, everything works offline.

What happens to my data if I change phones?

Turn on Google Drive sync, or export a backup file and import it on the new phone.

How accurate is the camera?

It reads standard target faces in good light and marks each arrow it finds. You can move any arrow with one touch before the end is saved.

Is the mental grade a scientific test?

No. It's an honest self-rating after every shot. Its value comes from doing it every time and seeing the pattern over weeks.
